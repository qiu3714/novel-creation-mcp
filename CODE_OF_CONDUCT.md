# Code of conduct

This project is maintained as a practical, source-aware open-source tool. Keep
discussion focused on reproducible issues, reviewable changes, and respectful
collaboration.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct, factual, and considerate when reporting problems or reviewing
  contributions.
- Respect maintainers, contributors, users, and people whose creative work is
  discussed by the project.
- Keep private manuscripts, credentials, personal data, and non-redistributable
  source material out of public issues and pull requests.
- Treat content-source, copyright, privacy, and security concerns as legitimate
  maintenance work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information, credentials, or private
  manuscript content.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors to accept unlicensed substantial
  excerpts, scraped datasets, or unsupported claims.
- Retaliation against someone for reporting a conduct, security, privacy, or
  content-source concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report ordinary conduct concerns with an issue or pull request comment. For
security, privacy, credential, or sensitive source-material concerns, follow
`SECURITY.md` and avoid public disclosure.

Maintainers may edit or hide comments, close threads, request changes, or block
participants when needed to protect project integrity. Enforcement should
preserve enough context to understand the decision while avoiding unnecessary
exposure of private or protected material.
