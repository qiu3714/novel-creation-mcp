# Content and source policy

This repository distributes software, original documentation, and data that
the project is entitled to redistribute. It is not a mirror for novels,
videos, scripts, paid courses, or other protected works.

## Allowed material

- Original project content.
- Public-domain or openly licensed material when the licence and attribution
  requirements are recorded.
- Short factual references and links used for commentary, without substantial
  copying.

## Not accepted without written permission

- Full or substantial excerpts from novels, videos, courses, subtitles, or
  platform posts.
- Proprietary character settings, plot summaries detailed enough to substitute
  for the source work, or datasets scraped from services that prohibit reuse.
- Personal data, account credentials, private manuscripts, or confidential
  prompts.

## Review rule

Every future knowledge-source import must record its source URL, licence or
reuse basis, date checked, and a concise statement of what was transformed.
When that evidence is unavailable, the material must not be added to the
public repository.

Current source-audit notes are kept in `SOURCE_AUDIT.md`. Unverified references
may be recorded there as risks or audit leads, but they must not be advertised
as bundled, redistributable project content until the review rule is satisfied.
